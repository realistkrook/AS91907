About this guide

Most writing about artificial intelligence falls into one of two traps. It either treats AI as an incomprehensible force that is about to arrive and change everything, or it dismisses the whole field as marketing. Neither is much use if you simply want to know what the software on your phone is doing.

The useful question is not whether machines can think, but what these particular systems can and cannot do.

So this guide takes a narrower approach. Every term is defined where it first appears. Every claim is one that could be checked. Where something is genuinely uncertain or disputed, it says so rather than picking a side and presenting it as settled.

The four sections are ordered so that each builds on the one before it, but they can be read in any order — anything that depends on an idea explained elsewhere links back to it.
