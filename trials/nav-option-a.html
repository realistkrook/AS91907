What to check

  • Can a first-time visitor see every page without clicking?
  • Narrow the window. Do the links wrap awkwardly or crowd the wordmark?
  • At phone width, how much vertical space has the header eaten?

The task to set testers

Give each tester the same instruction — “find the page about the history of AI” — and time them. Record the result in docs/nav-trial-notes.md.
